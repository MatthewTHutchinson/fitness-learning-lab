# Scope and safety

This repository is educational. It does not diagnose, treat, or provide
individual medical care. Training and supplement decisions depend on health,
medications, injury history, nutrition, and context not represented here.

Seek qualified medical or rehabilitation care for acute injury, severe or
worsening pain, neurological symptoms, unexplained systemic symptoms, major
loss of function, or uncertainty about safe participation. Discuss supplements
with a qualified clinician or pharmacist when medications, medical conditions,
pregnancy, or deficiency concerns are involved.
